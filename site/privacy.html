Privacy

Slow Drag Studio uses inquiry details submitted through the Begin form to understand the project and respond to the person who reached out.

What the inquiry form stores

  • Name, email, project need, timeline, and brief.
  • The page where the inquiry was submitted.
  • A timestamp created when the inquiry is saved.

How it is used

Inquiry details are used to review the request, reply, and keep a basic conversation archive. Slow Drag Studio does not sell inquiry details.

Site preferences

The site may remember the light or dark theme choice in browser local storage. The governed site state does not add analytics cookies or advertising pixels.

Contact

To ask about an inquiry already submitted, start a new conversation through the website and mention the earlier message.
